Lattice-based cryptography

🔐 GGH Cryptosystem

Play the protocol from any side. Alice generates a good and a bad basis for the same lattice, Bob encrypts with the bad one, and Eve — who only ever sees the bad one — tries her luck at the closest vector problem.
